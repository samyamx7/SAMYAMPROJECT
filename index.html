Chipmaking, in simple terms

A semiconductor is a material that can control electrical current. Engineers use it to build tiny switches called transistors.

Plan the circuit→2. Fabricate

Power devices

Modern chips can contain billions of transistors. Making them requires extremely precise equipment, clean rooms, specialized materials and expertise from companies across several countries. A finished chip is the result of a global supply chain, not just one factory.

What to watch next

These are important industry themes, not guarantees about future outcomes.

AI accelerators

Specialized processors help train and run AI models. Their performance depends not only on the processor, but also on memory, power use, networking and cooling.

High-bandwidth memory

HBM stacks memory close to high-performance processors, helping move large amounts of data quickly. It is increasingly important for AI systems.

Advanced packaging

Packaging connects multiple chiplets or dies into one system. As scaling becomes more difficult and expensive, packaging can improve performance and integration.

Supply-chain resilience

Governments and companies are investing in local production and diversified supply. Building advanced capacity takes years, skilled workers and major capital.
